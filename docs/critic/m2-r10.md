# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 10

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r10/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The kneeling payoff is the hardest frame to read** (MAJOR). The camera sat right behind the warrior, who
   stood between it and the back core: the core was hidden, the kneeling legs filled the frame, the STRIKE
   mark pointed at a grey block.
2. **Up close the golem turns into a heap of boulders** (MINOR). From afar a boxy block robot, not a ruin of
   columns and arches.
3. **The phase 3 SEALED label sits on the golem's face and explains too little** (MINOR).
4. **Glow effects hide the hero and blur the cyan rule** (MINOR). The core-hit bloom swallows the warrior; the
   cyan core light leaves a bright spot on the wet floor like a second core; dust puffs read as flat discs.
5. **Only half of the pitch's atmosphere shows up** (MINOR). No lightning in any frame; not flooded; the phase 3
   heart lights nothing on the floor.

What works: "the presentation is strong ... each phase has its own look, and the teaching layer makes the
one-weak-point rule and nearly every telegraph readable at a glance."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 10 -> round 11)

- Over-the-shoulder lock-on: the camera sits 0.75 m to the right of the warrior (1.7 m while the golem
  kneels), so the warrior is left of centre and never hides what it fights. The stagger camera rises to 5.2 m,
  golem parts within 5 m of the lens fade while it kneels, and the open back core's glow draws through
  everything.
- The golem (golem.py) carries a broken four-stone arch over its shoulders and stands on fluted column thighs:
  a ruin of arches and columns, not a block robot.
- SEALED sits below the heart with a plain, backed line: "strike it while the Ruin kneels".
- Core-hit flash smaller; the pooled cyan light is weaker and shorter (no second "core" on the floor); dust puffs
  are larger, softer and fade out with a cubic falloff.
- The heart's light sits low in front of the chest and is stronger (340, 420 open, slower falloff): molten
  light floods the floor where the fight happens. A second lightning strike lands 1.15 s into every
  phase-change roar. The outer ring of the arena, from just inside the colonnade to the walls, is flooded with
  shallow standing water that fades in at the shoreline.
