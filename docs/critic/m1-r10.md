# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 10

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r10/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The camera loses the colossus in phase 3** (MAJOR). The round 9 fix overcorrected: in the meteor
   rain the camera pointed at the floor and only the golem's shins showed; the leaping golem was a
   cropped chunk in a corner; the phase 2 roar ran an arm off the top. No burst chest or lava light.
2. **Stacked hazards turn into a mess of red and orange rings** (MINOR). Incoming hits (red) and burning
   ground (orange ring outline) shared shape and nearly the same hue; a half-faded burn ring gave no hint
   whether it still burned.
3. **Up close the golem is a heap of boxes** (MINOR). Greybox slabs, head and second arm hard to find.
4. **Stray shapes blur the weak-point rule** (MINOR). Shiny dark spheres (sealed cores) stick out of the
   body; small hollow cyan circles float on bare rock (HUD dots on cores hidden behind the body).
5. **None of the pitched atmosphere** (COSMETIC). No rain, puddles, lightning; boxy golem; cone flames.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 10 -> round 11)

- The player-focus framing is gone. During the meteor rain and the leap only the golem's torso and head
  count for the top of the frame (the raised arms may leave it), and the leap's landing ring is no longer
  a hard framing constraint (its near edge is right under the camera). During the leap the director asks
  for a low camera that looks up at the airborne golem. The phase-change roar asks for a wide shot.
- Burning ground is amber-gold (incoming hits stay red), its edge is a broken, jittered crack line rather
  than a clean circle, and it turns into dark cold cracks the moment it stops burning.
- The sealed back core is hidden until it opens; the HUD dots on other cores are removed.
- Phase 3 lava light: the molten heart is a strong warm point light that reaches the floor, and the fog
  turns ember-red with the phase 3 heat.
- The critic capture now waits for the phase-change roar to start (up to 30 s) before its shot.
