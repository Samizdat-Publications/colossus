# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 1

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r1/` (18 player-camera shots, `npm run playtest -- --scenario critic`).
The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The camera can't frame a colossus** (MAJOR). Up close the golem's head and raised arms leave the top
   of the frame (slam wind-up is a cropped slab), the stagger shot is jammed against dark rock, lock-on
   follows the leaping golem into the sky (half the screen black, no landing marker), tutorial box covers
   the warrior.
2. **Lock-on marks stone, and the loop is only taught after you die** (MAJOR). The reticle sits on the
   torso while the arm or back core glows elsewhere; players will read the diamond as "hit here". The
   line that teaches the loop only appears on the death screen.
3. **Glow is everywhere, so "glowing" means nothing** (MAJOR). Cores look different in every shot (black
   orb, flat stickers, white blooms); the same orange is used for eyes, braziers, rings, fissure, break
   meter and the phase 3 chest; bloom washes out the HUD.
4. **Telegraphs don't form a consistent visual language** (MINOR). Red vs orange rings, shockwave reads
   as a raised cream platform, "about to hit" and "already burning" look the same, rocks invisible in
   flight, meteors show as overlapping rings with no rocks.
5. **The kill and the death are unclear moments** (MINOR). Golem still upright at zero health, unlabeled
   "26" next to the bar, combat HUD visible behind the victory screen, thin grey subtitle, player death
   with HP bar still showing about 60% (instant-kill test artifact plus the damage trail).

Cosmetic, not ranked: no rain, lightning, water or puddles yet (milestone 3).

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 1 -> round 2)

- Camera: lock-on framing constraint keeps the golem's highest point and the player's feet in frame
  (extra look-up plus extra FOV); golem fragments near the camera dither out; lock point is clamped and
  ignores the leap (camera looks at the landing ring); tips moved under the boss bar.
- Lock-on reticle now marks the best reachable core (small markers on the other open cores); the first
  punish windows show "Its fist is stuck: strike the glowing core!" with a pulsing marker on the core.
- Colour language: cores are cyan (the only cyan in the game), eyes amber, incoming-attack telegraphs red,
  burning ground orange. Bloom reduced; core lights reduced; HUD gets dark backing.
- Telegraphs: every incoming impact uses the same red ring with a filling disc; the stomp shockwave is a
  thin bright ring band; thrown rocks and meteors glow and leave trails.
- Death: the golem crumbles back into rubble at zero health; the boss damage number is labelled; the
  combat HUD hides behind end screens.
