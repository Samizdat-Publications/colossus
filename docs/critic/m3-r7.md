# Critic report: milestone 3 (feel), round 7

Screenshots: `test-output/m3-critic-r7/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The only hit that matters shows no blade contact** (MAJOR). The swing resolved on the first frame of the
   strike, when the blade was still out to the side, so the burst appeared away from the blade.
2. **At the shin the camera loses the warrior, and the deflect happens on cyan-lit stone** (MAJOR).
3. **The roll's invulnerability starts before the roll animation does** (MAJOR). EVADED over an upright warrior.
4. **Heavy impacts have no mass** (MINOR). Thin slam dust, an unreadable collapse, a floating crumble.
5. **The warrior's own attacks and burn damage feel light** (MINOR). Hard-edged trails; confetti chips around fire.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 7 -> round 8)

- Swings resolve mid-arc (40% into the strike window) and the core burst sits at the point of the blade nearest the
  core (`Player.bladeNear`).
- A core whose bone is faded out of the view dims with it (emissive x0.2, halo x0.15); the pooled cyan light only
  lights the stone when the core is marked, just hit, or the back core is open.
- Slams raise a column of dust and throw heavier chunks; the crumbling golem's stones accelerate as they fall.
- Softer sword trails; the burning-ground edge chips are gone; steam hisses off a burning warrior; a stronger
  burning glow at the bottom of the screen; a bigger roll splash and dust puff.
- Capture: the core-hit beat uses a real click on a fresh slam (hazards cleared); the shot log records the
  warrior's state and time in state.

Not fixed (see HANDOFF.md): the roll. Its animation step blends from the pre-roll pose with a linear weight, so the
somersault is only partly shown. A full-strength version (the new `Step.blendIn`) also needs the rotation pivot
raised, or the body sinks into the floor mid-roll; it was tried and reverted before the handoff.
