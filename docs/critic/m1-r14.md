# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 14

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r14/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The camera loses the boss up close, worst in phase 3** (MAJOR; round 13 rated the same issue MINOR).
   The meteor frame was taken while the camera was still settling after the roar's shove: legs and hips
   only. The leap cut the fists; a slam wind-up raised an arm out of frame.
2. **Weak points are hard to see outside punish windows** (MINOR). Out-of-reach arm cores were dimmed to
   small mint dots; the phase 3 heart has no "can I hit it?" cue on screen.
3. **Phase 3's red wash drowns out the danger colour** (MINOR). Pink-maroon sky and floor.
4. **Burning ground does not read as fire** (MINOR). No flame or smoke yet (milestone 3).
5. **The warrior gets lost in the big moments** (MINOR). The kill shot did not show the warrior.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 14 -> round 15)

- The director frames the whole meteor attack wide (3.5 m of pull-back from its wind-up, faster
  framing while the warrior is shoved), so the camera is already in place when the rubble falls.
- The leap framing includes the fists.
- Arm cores out of reach glow steady and clearly (0.8 of full), reachable ones steady and brighter,
  STRIKE ones pulse; spent cores vanish when the golem dies.
- The phase 3 lava reflection on the clouds is amber, not pink-red, so red warning rings keep contrast.
- The victory shot is high and wide over the warrior's shoulder: the warrior in the foreground, the
  collapse beyond.
