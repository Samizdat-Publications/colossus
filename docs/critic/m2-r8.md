# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 8

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r8/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **You can't tell which orange things burn** (MINOR). Phase 3's glowing flooded joints and sunken tiles read
   as flat amber tiles and orange rings; the real burning ground looked like gold sparkle (the flask colour).
2. **Up close the golem turns into a heap of blocks** (MINOR). The stagger, the leaning double slam and the
   death shot show walls of blocks without a readable head or pose.
3. **The colossus doesn't look colossal in normal play** (MINOR). Two to three warrior heights on screen from
   the standard camera; boxy arms and a visor-slit face read as a robot.
4. **Phases 1 and 2 don't look like night** (MINOR). A pale sky glow and bright grey flagstones; no lightning
   in the frames; rain rings like polka dots.
5. **Cutscene slips** (MINOR). The phase 2 roar looked headless (head tipped back behind the chest), the lock-on
   marker floated on a fist in the cut, the intro roar had no visible eyes, a grey core showed through the
   death stats, damage numbers piled onto the STRIKE tag, the cape stuck out like a plank.

What works: "the teaching layer is strong ... one UI style from the title to both end screens make it look
like one finished game."

Worst remaining issue severity: MINOR

## Fixes (round 8 -> round 9)

- The glowing water is gone (any glow in the ring-shaped joints read as rings or tiles). Phase 3's light comes
  from the golem: its veins, the molten heart's light and the firelight of burning ground.
- Burning ground is fire: a pool of flame billboards (the brazier flame shader, dimmer, deeper orange and wider)
  stands on every burning patch, with firelight on the stone; the decal is fire orange, not gold; fewer embers,
  more smoke.
- Night: moonlight 2.3 to 1.85, hemisphere 1.15 to 0.95, half the sky glow around the moon, darker clouds;
  rain rings fade out beyond 5 to 13 m and vary in size.
- Roars keep the face toward the warrior (head tilts forward, not back); the phase cut sits higher (4.5 to 5.7 m)
  so it sees the face; the reticle, core markers and damage numbers hide during the cut.
- Death camera: picks the side with fewer golem limbs near the lens, and the line-of-sight fade stays on in
  the death shots; the stats backing is nearly opaque; knockback no longer blows the cape out flat.
- Damage numbers float beside the STRIKE tag instead of on it; the lock-on camera sits 0.4 m lower so the golem
  looms more.
