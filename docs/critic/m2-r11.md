# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 11

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r11/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Phase 3 climax does not read** (MAJOR). The strong floor light painted the whole golem salmon, so the
   burst chest showed no molten core; the SEALED tag was hard to read and contradicted "bursts open"; the
   meteor call and the leap were pinned against the top edge; red rings lost contrast on the red-lit floor.
2. **The golem turns into a heap of blocks when it attacks** (MINOR).
3. **The warrior and his attacks barely register** (MINOR). A hairline sword, a board-like cape.
4. **Death has no impact** (MINOR). No vignette or knockdown at zero health; the FALLEN overlay hid the body.
5. **Phase 2 change and weather effects look cheap** (COSMETIC). An unbranched bolt; the thrown rock a black
   disc with an orange halo.

What works: "for a browser game it looks finished ... a first-time player can follow the whole loop from the
shots alone: slam, stuck fist, STRIKE, BREAK, DOWN, back core."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 11 -> round 12)

- Phase 3: the burst chest holds a big, white-hot molten core (scale 1.3, pulsing), the brightest thing on the
  golem; body veins are a little calmer. The heart's light stays at the chest (lights the stone around it) and
  a soft molten pool glows on the floor around the golem's feet (a radial glow, no hot spot). The SEALED tag
  is gone; a tip explains the heart while you are locked on. Ring rims brighten on the lava-lit floor.
- Framing: the meteor call and the leap keep 16% clear sky above the golem (director top margin); the meteor
  call widens 4 m with up to 6.5 m of pull-back.
- Death: a dark red vignette closes in while dying; the FALLEN overlay is lighter so the body shows; the cape
  may drape past its shoulder clamp when the warrior falls.
- Warrior (warrior.py): a broader, longer blade with a cold steel glint (it heats to orange while a heavy
  charges); the cape's rest shape is pleated, deeper toward the hem.
- Lightning forks into two or three thinner branches; thrown rocks and meteors use the golem's stone (moonlit
  rim and veins) instead of a flat dark material.
