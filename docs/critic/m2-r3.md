# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 3

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r3/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Up close, the golem is a wall of boxes** (MAJOR). In punish windows the golem filled the frame as
   one mass of grey cubes and balls with the same blue highlight on every edge; torso and arms could not
   be told apart. "From a distance (03, 04, 12) the outline works."
2. **The camera loses the boss during its big attacks** (MAJOR). Raised arm off the top in the slam
   wind-up, the meteor rain and the leap (captured with the 78-degree lens ceiling from round 1).
3. **The "glowing cores" rule vs other glows** (MINOR). Orange eyes, orange cracks, the molten chest; the
   amber seal runes looked like burning ground.
4. **The big moments fall flat** (MINOR). Thin phase 2 cracks at a distance; the victory crumble reads as
   the model coming apart; no rock visible in the death shot.
5. **The arena does not look like a flooded ruin at night** (COSMETIC). No puddles, dusk-like light, flat
   mountains, hard-edged dark clouds, glints on the floor like road markings.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 3 -> round 4)

- Stone reflects much less of the sky (the blue edge on every bevel came from the environment map and a
  blue sky light); the sky light is less blue.
- The forearms are rough dark boulders now, so the arms separate from the dressed-stone torso.
- Close to the giant the camera drops low and looks up (it used to rise): the golem fits the frame and
  reads as huge. Probes at 7 m and 15 m keep its head in frame through slam, meteor rain and leap.
- Phase 2 veins: larger cells and brighter, readable across the arena.
- The seal runes are faint cold sigils during the fight (they only blaze while the golem assembles).
- Softer floor glints, lighter and more translucent clouds.
- Milestone 3 work has started and is in this build: rain, splashes, lightning with thunder, brazier
  fire, embers and smoke over burning ground, dust and debris on impacts, sparks, a sword trail, the
  golem flinching when a core is hit and its head following the warrior.
