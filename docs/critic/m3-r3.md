# Critic report: milestone 3 (feel), round 3

Screenshots: `test-output/m3-critic-r3/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Getting hit has no physical weight** (MAJOR). The body untouched at the moment of impact; the recoil pose with
   both arms thrown up read as cheering; no rock visible at the hit.
2. **The shockwave roll drowns in glow** (MAJOR). Ring bloom, burning ground and the burn glow stacked into a haze.
3. **The slam lands like a pillow** (MINOR). No cracks at once, thin dust.
4. **The warrior's own attacks feel weightless** (MINOR). Straight legs through the combo; the heavy release lacked
   a payoff.
5. **The collapse to its knees reads as a heap** (MINOR). The lock-on diamond on a hidden core.

What feels good: "the core hit is the best moment ... the weather sells the arena ... the gold charge glow on the
heavy attack reads well."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 3 -> round 4)

- Hits: the warrior's armour flashes red-hot for about a third of a second (a shader term on the warrior's
  materials), the spark burst is larger, and the recoil throws the torso and head back with the arms flung down and
  out and the knees buckling (no raised arms).
- The shockwave ring and band glow less, so the warrior shows through them.
- Slams: the cracks appear at once as pale grit in the broken stone; the dust wave and cloud are denser.
- Attacks: light strikes step into the blow on a bent front knee; heavy strikes kick the camera.
- While the golem is down, the lock-on diamond only shows on the core the STRIKE tag marks (never on a hidden core).
- Balance: 4 decent-bot wins in 20 across the last two checks, most losses late in phase 3 with the golem below 20%;
  golem health 1850 to 1750.
