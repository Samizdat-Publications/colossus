## Closing milestone 1

Sixteen rounds took the greybox from BLOCKER-level problems to a stable loop: every attack is
telegraphed on the floor, hits only land inside their warnings, the damage loop is taught in order, and
both end screens work. Round 13 rated the worst issue MINOR; rounds 14 to 16 keep returning to one
theme at MAJOR: a lock-on camera behind a 1.8 m warrior cannot keep a 14.5 m golem's head, its raised
arms and the warning rings at the warrior's feet in one frame at close range. The critics agree the
rings keep every attack readable ("the floor rings still carry the gameplay information").

Camera work is part of milestone 3 (Feel), and the greybox boxes themselves cause part of the
unreadability ("heap of slabs"), which milestone 2 replaces. So milestone 1 closes here and the framing
item moves to milestone 3, where the self-test loop continues with it on the list.

## Carried into milestone 2 and 3

- Warrior accent colour: ivory cloth instead of red, so the warrior never matches a danger marker.
- Less wide-lens stretching: a lower field-of-view ceiling with more pull-back.
- Framing of the golem's head and wind-ups at close range (milestone 3 camera pass).
