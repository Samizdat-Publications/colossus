# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 1

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r1/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch (told that
rain, lightning, fire, particles and the final menus come later).

1. **The camera loses the warrior at close range** (MAJOR). At the stomp the warrior was below the boss
   bar (knocked back while the framing lagged); fists and head cropped in the meteor rain and the leap.
2. **From behind, the warrior is a white board** (MAJOR). A rigid ivory cape from shoulders to knees, the
   brightest object on screen, hiding the sword arm; stiff as a plank when he falls.
3. **The golem has no face and its silhouette falls apart** (MINOR). Faint eyes, a bright blue rim on
   every block ("glowing plastic"), one crack pattern repeated on every block, the orange chest.
4. **The arena does not look drowned** (MINOR). Dry matte flagstones; missing stones are black pits; the
   mountains are flat cut-outs.
5. **Clutter along the bottom edge** (MINOR). Specular glints blooming into orbs on the boss bar; the
   controls card.

What works: the ruin (arcade, pillars, ring floor), tips, STRIKE and diamond markers, BREAK/DOWN, the
colour-coded warnings, both end screens. "The damage loop is understandable from the screenshots alone."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 1 -> round 2)

- The lock-on framing enforces its hard constraint without lag: if a knockback or a camera push would
  drop the warrior's feet (or a ring they stand in) below the frame, the pitch follows at once.
- The cape is a verlet cloth (the Blender grid, top row pinned to the chest, body spheres, gusty wind),
  shortened to mid-thigh and dyed weathered bone: warm against the cold stone but not the brightest
  thing on screen.
- Golem eyes 2x brighter and larger; the moonlit rim on the stone cut to a hint; every stone piece
  samples its own patch of the vein map.
- Wet stone: flagstones are glossier (roughness halved) and reflect the moonlit environment; the water
  has a real sheen and raindrop ripples in its normals.
- Bloom threshold above 1, so only real glow (cores, fire, eyes) blooms, not armour glints; dimmer fill
  light on the warrior; the controls card lists Esc and fades after 12 s.
