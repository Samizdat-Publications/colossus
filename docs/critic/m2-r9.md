# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 9

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r9/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **"Glowing" draws the eye to the wrong places** (MINOR). Arm cores are cyan specks at fighting distance
   while the orange eyes are the brightest glow; the blazing phase 3 chest never says if or when it can be hit;
   joint water reflecting orange light reads close to burning ground.
2. **Up close, the golem stops looking like a creature** (MINOR). The stagger view is dominated by a pale arm;
   one fist of the two-fisted slam; the victory collapse swallows the warrior.
3. **The warrior looks like a mannequin with a cardboard cape** (MINOR). Thin tube limbs, a flat tan cape.
4. **Core hits don't look like they land** (MINOR). A low flat slash under a core at helmet height.
5. **The storm and phase 3 lighting fall short of the pitch** (MINOR). Clouds with hard straight edges; phase 3
   darker and browner than phase 1.

Cosmetic: identical white flame cones; round puddle reflections like wisps; the seal runes like a dashed
selection circle; the Cinzel Q on the controls card; "Damage taken" without a unit.

What works: "the danger signals are clear and stay consistent through all three phases ... the title screen,
the rubble rising in the intro, the orange-cracked phase 2 roar and both end screens look like a finished
Souls-style game."

Worst remaining issue severity: MINOR

## Fixes (round 9 -> round 10)

- Cores: larger (1.35 to 1.65) with a soft cyan halo sprite that follows the core's glow; the golem's eyes are
  dimmer (3.2 to 2.1) and are now rough ember sockets under the brow instead of box slits (golem.py).
- Phase 3: the sealed heart carries a "SEALED / bring it to its knees" label for the first 30 s of phase 3.
- Water and puddles are rougher (fewer point-light glints); the seal runes glow much less.
- Stagger: the braced arm plants in front of the knee instead of out to the side; the line-of-sight fade also
  runs in the victory collapse.
- Warrior (warrior.py): heroic proportions: broader pauldrons with three lames, heavier plate on arms and legs,
  larger gauntlets and sabatons, wider tabard, taller crest, fuller cape; the cape has a dark hem and dirt
  toward the bottom.
- Swings aim: when the target core is above the chest, the warrior leans back and lifts the sword arm so the
  blade meets the core.
- Sky: cloud noise octaves are rotated (plain value noise lined up with its lattice into straight edges).
- Flames on burning ground: fewer, varied in size, deeper orange and dimmer. Controls card keys use the text
  face in small caps. The victory stat reads "Health lost ... HP".
