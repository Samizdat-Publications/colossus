# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 11

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r11/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The phase 3 camera loses the boss and cuts off its warnings** (MAJOR). The leaping golem showed only
   from the chest down; its landing ring ran under the boss panel; meteor rings and the raised fist of
   the roar touched the panel and the tip strip.
2. **Up close, the golem is an unreadable pile of boxes** (MINOR). Greybox slabs; the warrior's grey armour
   blends into the grey stone.
3. **Too many warm colours on the floor** (MINOR). Burning ground sat next to a pale-orange shockwave of
   nearly the same colour; red rings lost contrast on lava-lit stone; marks slid under the boss panel.
4. **Which glow can I hit?** (MINOR). Reachable cores glowed without a STRIKE tag when a warning was near;
   out-of-reach cores glowed the same as reachable ones; the phase 3 heart is a small red bead.
5. **The atmosphere from the pitch is missing** (COSMETIC). No rain, water or lightning; untextured boxes;
   brazier flames look like floating triangles.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 11 -> round 12)

- A probe (`scripts/probe-cam.mjs`) showed the lock-on framing lagging the leap: the golem reaches its
  6 m apex in about 0.3 s, faster than the camera's pitch and field of view could follow. The director
  now frames the apex before the jump (the golem's top is lifted by the apex height during the wind-up
  and the rise) and the camera follows faster during the leap.
- The boss panel is lower, thinner and see-through (no dark backdrop), and the bottom framing margin
  drops from 21% to 16% of the view, so rings near the bottom stay visible.
- STRIKE marks an arm core whenever it is within sword reach (the teaching tip still waits for a calm
  moment); arm cores out of reach glow dim, in reach they burn bright and pulse.
- Shockwaves are pale blue-white (burning ground stays amber, incoming hits red); red warning rings get a
  thin bright rim that holds on lava-lit stone; the phase 3 floor tint is gentler.
- The critic capture forces each phase change (`forcePhase`) and the kill (`killGolem`), so the roar and
  victory shots no longer depend on how much damage the bot dealt.
