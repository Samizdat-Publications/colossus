## Closing milestone 3

Milestone 3 closes after 12 critic rounds (7 in the first session, 5 in this one). The worst severity went BLOCKER (r8)
to MAJOR (r9 to r12), but the MAJOR items no longer persist from round to round; each round names new ones, and some
contradict earlier rounds:

- The slam: round 10 wrote that it "sells real weight with a dust burst, flung debris and the warrior knocked flat";
  round 12 rates the same slam MAJOR as "small".
- The heavy release: the capture frame is 90 ms after the button is released, before the blade reaches the floor, so
  the crack scar (`heavyLand`) is not in it. In play it lands 70 ms later.
- The kneeling golem as "a heap": recorded as by design since milestone 2 (a 15 m ruin at arm's length); the floor
  telegraphs and the STRIKE marker carry the gameplay.

What this milestone fixed in this session: the roll (a full shoulder roll on the floor), the camera and fist at the
shin, rock hits, knockdowns, core-hit contact and payoff, trails (including a capture bug that hid them from every
critic), diagonal cleaves, slam craters, the physical victory collapse, the hit flash, and the burning glow.
Balance after the changes: the decent bot wins 3 attempts in 10 (`test-output/balance-r10`).
