# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 2

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r2/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The "down" state is a shapeless rubble pile** (MAJOR). The stagger pose bent the torso almost flat,
   so the golem read as a heap with no head, knees or arms, and the back core was not visible from the
   front. It looked like the intro rubble and the death collapse.
2. **The golem stops reading when it crouches or cracks** (MINOR). Near-black fists on a dark floor; a
   cyan bloom flare swallowing the fist at the punish; an even crack web in phase 2; a box head with a
   visor slit that reads as a robot.
3. **The phase 3 camera crops the boss** (MINOR).
4. **Hazard decals look like stickers** (MINOR). Flat amber starbursts (fire comes in milestone 3).
5. **Cluttered hit point, weak BREAK gauge** (MINOR). Milestone 4 (UI).

Cosmetic: brazier flames are flat triangles, flat mountains, little visible water.

What works: slam, rock and leap rings, the tips, the end screens.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 2 -> round 3)

- A new kneel: torso up (spine and chest bent 32 degrees instead of 74), head bowed but visible, fists
  planted in front. It reads as a giant on its knees.
- While it is down, a shaft of cyan light rises over the core worth striking, visible from any side;
  with the STRIKE tag and the cyan floor ring it answers "where is behind it?".
- The head is a craggy boulder skull with a heavy brow, cheek stones, a jaw and crown stones instead of
  a dressed block; the fists and other rough stone are lighter; each piece gets its own crack coverage in
  phase 2; the marked core's glow is gentler so it does not swallow the fist.
- The cliffs are denser, with a second, taller ridge behind them.
