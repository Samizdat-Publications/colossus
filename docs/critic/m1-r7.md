# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 7

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r7/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The stagger payoff is a camera wreck** (MAJOR). The camera jams into the kneeling golem; three bright
   spots compete (back core, a flash, an arm core); a burning crack runs under the boss bar.
2. **The camera loses the golem in phases 2 and 3** (MAJOR). Meteor rain points at the floor (legs only);
   the phase 3 chest is never visible; the phase 2 roar crops both raised fists.
3. **Unlit teal balls blur the "only glowing cores" rule** (MINOR). Dim cores in the title rubble, flying
   during assembly, on the lower torso in phase 2.
4. **Deaths don't explain themselves** (MINOR). No ring, projectile or burst in the dying frame; generic tip.
5. **The atmosphere from the pitch is missing** (COSMETIC). No rain, lightning, water; flat boxes; flat
   brazier flames; phase 3 lava light only tints the sky.

What works: the red danger language, STRIKE markers, banners and BREAK/DOWN bar, both end screens.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 7 -> round 8)

- Only warning rings the warrior stands in (or is about to) are hard framing constraints; the meteor rain
  used to drag the view to the floor with every ring in the area.
- More field of view for giant poses (up to 84), pull-back up to 4 m; the director raises the camera 3.5 m
  during a stagger (and 1.5 m in the meteor rain) so the kneeling golem is seen from above.
- Arm cores dim to a third while the golem is down so the back core (or phase 3 heart) is the obvious
  target; sealed cores are dark stone sockets, hidden until they ignite at the roar.
- The killing blow freezes for a beat and the death screen names the killer ("Killed by a thrown rock").
- Phase 3 warms the floor with lava light (hemisphere ground colour).
