# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 5

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r5/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Your warrior disappears when it matters** (MAJOR). In most action shots the warrior was a 30 to 50 px
   figure in dark steel on dark wet stone; in the stomp dodge it was a speck between the shockwave and a
   burning splat; at the moment of death dust puffs hid the body completely.
2. **Up close, the golem melts into a rubble heap** (MINOR). Slam recovery, sweep, stagger and the death
   collapse all read as the same hunched mass; the stagger did not look like "collapses to its knees".
3. **The phase changes don't land** (MINOR). The phase 2 roar was seen from low and far through rain with
   thin, faint cracks; phase 3 "lava light" was a brown sepia haze; the dying golem lost its phase 3 look.
4. **The controls card stays on screen for all of phase 1** (MINOR). A 195 x 285 px card at the upper
   right, with a dim "H hide this card" line.
5. **The ground warnings look like stickers** (COSMETIC). Flat, unlit paint on a lit scene.

What works: "the only cyan cores can be hurt rule, the step-by-step tips, the ground telegraphs and both end
screens are all clear. The intro sells the ruin rising. Nothing here stops a first-time player."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 5 -> round 6)

- Measured first: the critic scenario now logs the warrior's on-screen height, camera distance and FOV for
  every shot. The worst shots had the warrior 55 to 60 px tall with the camera 15 to 20 m away.
- Warrior light (`src/render/heroLight.ts`): a camera-side fill and a cool rim added to the warrior's
  materials only, both growing with camera distance. The warrior now reads as a pale figure at any range.
- Lock-on camera: FOV cap 78 to 72 degrees, automatic pull-back 5 to 3 m (5 in the leap), close-range extra
  distance 1.6 to 0.8 m, director widening cut (stagger 4.5 to 3, meteor 3.5 to 2.5, leap 4 to 3).
- Dust thins out along the line of sight from the camera to the warrior (never hides them).
- Death camera: a low view 5 to 7 m from the fallen warrior, the golem looming above, rising slowly
  (warrior about 200 px tall instead of 57).
- Phase change: a hard cut to a three-quarter view of the whole roaring golem (clear of pillars), then a
  blend back; the veins flare during the roar and are wider and brighter afterwards; the eyes flare too.
- Phase 3: the flooded joints between the flagstones glow deep molten red; the sky darkens with a red
  glow on the horizon; fog and far ridges go dark (silhouettes, not terracotta).
- Victory: the veins, eyes and molten heart keep burning while the golem falls and bleed out over 2 to 3 s.
- Stagger: a new asymmetric kneel (one knee down, the other up, one fist braced, the other arm over the
  knee, head hanging) and dazed, flickering eyes.
- Controls card: a compact two-column card (about 330 x 150 px) that hides after 10 s and leaves an
  "H controls" pill.
- Found while probing the stagger: every core light was parented to its core mesh, so a core hiding or
  appearing changed the scene's light count and recompiled every shader (1.4 to 2.2 s freezes at the intro
  roar, the first stagger and phase 3). Lights now live in the scene with constant count, and a warm-up
  compiles and renders everything once behind the loading screen. Worst first-use frame: 2.2 s to 0.13 s.
