# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 3

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r3/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The camera ends up inside the golem** (MAJOR). Phase 2 double slam shows only close-up cracked stone
   and sky (warrior, floor and fissure out of view); a dotted see-through forearm fills the core-hit shot;
   the throwing arm fills half the rock shot.
2. **The HUD covers the ground you dodge on** (MAJOR). Warrior's legs and the centre of the red rings sit
   behind the bottom boss panel; the controls card stays up for all of phase 1.
3. **Phases 2 and 3 muddle the colours** (MAJOR). The same orange crack pattern covers every surface,
   arm cores get lost, the meteor is a laser-like beam and a flat orange gem, the golem turns pink-white.
4. **Deaths have no visible cause, and the tip names the wrong colour** (MINOR). "Orange landing rings"
   but every landing ring is red; camera pressed into scenery behind the death screen.
5. **The storm from the pitch never shows up** (COSMETIC). No rain, lightning or standing water; victory
   collapse looks like a floating column; HUD still up during the collapse.

What works: the damage loop is taught well (context prompts, STRIKE markers, damage numbers, BREAK meter,
"It is down!" prompt); both end screens are clear.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 3 -> round 4)

- Lock-on camera rewritten as a framing solve: each frame it picks pitch and field of view so that the
  warrior's feet stay above the boss panel (bottom 21% kept clear) and the golem's highest point stays
  in frame, pulling back when both cannot fit. The camera never moves into the golem; golem parts that
  crowd the lens or block the warrior fade smoothly (whole-part alpha, not a dither pattern).
- Phase veins are sparse and patchy (larger cells, a coverage mask), no uniform heat wash, cores get
  brighter with each phase so they stay the brightest thing on the golem.
- Meteor and rock trails are short tapered comet trails; rocks are dark stone with an ember glow.
- Death camera rises over the fallen warrior with the golem behind; death hint says red rings; boss
  panel hides during the victory collapse; controls card shows for 25 s; lock-on tip hides on death.
