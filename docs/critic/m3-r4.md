# Critic report: milestone 3 (feel), round 4

Screenshots: `test-output/m3-critic-r4/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The rock hits with no rock** (MAJOR). The boulder vanished at contact with only specks and a faint puff.
2. **The hurt flash turns the warrior into a pink mannequin** (MINOR). A flat salmon fill, too long.
3. **Blade contact is not sold** (MINOR). Several frames were captured before the strike (no trail yet).
4. **The colossal moments are undersized** (MINOR). The stagger frames came before the knees landed; the death showed
   an intact figure (the crumble had not started); the death camera cut off the raised fist.
5. **Fire and lightning look like stickers** (MINOR). The burning-ground frame came before the patch burned.

What feels good: "the weather sells the fantasy ... the slam is the best-built impact in the set ... the core hit
answers with a hot cyan-white flash."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 4 -> round 5)

- A thrown rock or a meteor that hits now shatters where it hits: ten large fragments carry on in its direction and
  a burst of grit hangs at the impact height (not only at the floor).
- The hit flash is a brief hot rim on the warrior's silhouette (0.22 s), not a flat fill.
- The golem starts to fall apart after 0.5 s of death (was 0.8 s) and crumbles over 2.1 s; the victory camera stands
  further back and higher so the flung arms stay in frame.
- Capture timing (a test artifact, not the game): combo and heavy frames now land mid-strike; the stagger burst
  starts after the knees land; the burning-ground frame waits for a burning patch and puts the warrior in it (hazards
  expose a `burning` flag to the test hooks).
- Balance check at golem health 1750: 3 decent-bot wins in 10.
