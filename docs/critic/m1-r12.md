# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 12

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r12/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Phase 3 camera hides the new attacks** (MAJOR). In the meteor rain (the warrior at the golem's feet)
   the head and the falling rock were cut off; the near half of the leap ring was below the screen edge.
2. **STRIKE says "go" while the floor says "run"** (MAJOR). The STRIKE tag showed on reachable cores while
   a sweep, a shockwave or a fissure was live.
3. **The stagger reward is an unreadable heap** (MAJOR). Camera pressed against the kneeling golem, pale
   overexposed slabs, three competing glows (hit flash, marked core, dimmed arm core), no hint which side
   is "behind".
4. **Hazards do not share one visual language** (MINOR). Flat burn decals, white shockwave vs red threats.
5. **The warrior's own state is hard to read** (MINOR). Roll and knockdown look alike; a flare on the helmet.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 12 -> round 13)

- The meteor rain opens with a roar that shoves the warrior back (11 m, no damage, with a warning ring),
  and the rubble starts falling a moment later around where the warrior landed. With room between them
  the camera frames the golem's torso and the rings around the warrior.
- The leap ring is a framing constraint again (the low camera and apex anticipation make it fit), with up
  to 7 m of pull-back allowed during the leap.
- STRIKE appears only when a reachable core is in an open window and no warning ring, sweep, fissure or
  shockwave is on its way to the warrior. Reachable cores otherwise glow steady (not pulsing), cores out of
  reach glow dim.
- The stagger: camera 5 m higher and 3.5 m wider, arm cores hidden while it is down (only the core worth
  hitting glows), a cyan ring on the floor under that core ("stand here"), softer hit flashes, a dimmer
  warrior fill light and a darker greybox stone.
