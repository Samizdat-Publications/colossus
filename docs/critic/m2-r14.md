## Fixes (round 14 -> round 15)

- Bug: the sword trail joined each new swing to where the previous swing ended (the quad between a fresh sample and
  a stale one drew a streak across the arena). A new swing now starts a fresh trail.
- Victory: the dying golem reels back with its arms flung wide and topples away from the warrior before it crumbles;
  no see-through fade during the victory; a lighter victory overlay so the rubble shows.
- Death camera: high over the body (3.4 to 5.2 m), stricter clearance from golem limbs, a faster glide, and the yaw
  offset corrected for the steep pitch, so the fallen body lies in the left or right third next to the FALLEN text.
- Phase 3: the stone stays grey with glowing veins (much less warm ground light); the heart churns with drifting
  molten veins and darker crusts (a shader), and meteors show in the sky before they fall.
- The thrown rock's glow is smaller; the leap keeps 20% sky above the golem.
