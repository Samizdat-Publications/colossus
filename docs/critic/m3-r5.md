# Critic report: milestone 3 (feel), round 5

Screenshots: `test-output/m3-critic-r5/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Getting hit by a giant barely registers, and the rock hit is unreadable** (MAJOR). The warrior never left their
   feet at a knockdown slam; the salmon tint read as a shader glitch; the rock fragments burst above the warrior and
   read as far away.
2. **Core hits do not connect with the blade** (MINOR).
3. **The warrior's attacks have no commitment, and the heavy has no release** (MINOR).
4. **Under the golem, the camera buries the deflect** (MINOR). The capture struck the shin from between the legs.
5. **The big set pieces land without mass** (MINOR). No dust as the golem crumbles; the shockwave lifts nothing.

What feels good: "the weather does its job ... the core hit is the best moment in the set ... the slam's dust wall
and flying chips have real heft."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 5 -> round 6)

- Knockdowns throw the warrior: a short toss off their feet before the fall.
- The hit flash is white-hot and lasts a few frames (about 0.1 s), not a pink tint.
- A rock that hits the warrior bursts at the warrior's body.
- The heavy blow flares at the blade as it lands (a gold flash and sparks) and kicks the camera.
- The crumbling golem throws up dust (and a rumble of stones) as its pieces reach the floor; the shockwave front
  lifts more dust and spray.
- Capture: the deflect beat strikes the shin from outside the legs.
- The transparent variant of the golem's fading materials is compiled behind the loading screen: the first leap and
  the first stagger near the lens used to stall for up to a second while it compiled.
