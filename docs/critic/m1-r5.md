# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 5

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r5/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The camera loses the golem when it matters most** (MAJOR). During the leap only a slab of golem pokes
   in behind the HUD; the thrown rock is not on screen; the stagger is a wall of grey boxes; the death
   shots show only legs.
2. **Phase 3 contradicts the core rule** (MINOR). The biggest glowing thing is an orange orb on the chest
   that is not listed as a target.
3. **Hints point at the wrong threat** (MINOR). "The cracked floor burns" while the warrior stands in a red
   sweep zone; STRIKE marker shown while standing on the fissure lane.
4. **Hint box and controls card sit where the fight happens** (MINOR).
5. **The killing blow doesn't read as a kill** (MINOR). Golem crouches with cores still lit.

Cosmetic: no rain/puddles/lightning, greybox floor, a teal sphere flying in during assembly, the roar
shot hides the head, cyan damage numbers on a cyan glow.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 5 -> round 6)

- Camera "wide" requests from the director: the leap pulls back 9 m (with a lower 6 m apex), a stagger 3 m,
  the victory 6 m, with extra field of view; the death camera stands back from the midpoint of the warrior
  and the golem so both are in frame; rocks carry a bright halo so they read in flight.
- The phase 3 chest core is a real weak point: a cyan crystal in a lava-lit cavity, hittable (critical)
  while the golem is down, reachable from the front between its knees; the stagger tip names it.
- Tips and the STRIKE marker stay quiet while a warning covers the warrior (only stagger tips get through);
  tips sit in a one-line strip at the top (the camera keeps that strip free of golem); the controls card
  moved to the bottom-right corner and shows for 15 s.
- On the killing blow every core goes dark within 0.25 s and the crumble starts at 0.8 s.
- Damage numbers are white with a dark outline.
- Sanitize pass rewritten without `isnan()` (D3D may fold it away): comparisons plus D3D max() semantics.
