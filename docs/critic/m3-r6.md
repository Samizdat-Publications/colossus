Screenshots: `test-output/m3-critic-r6/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Rolling through the shockwave looks like getting hit** (MAJOR). The capture's roll came a frame too late (a
   100 ms poll), so the ring knocked the warrior down; the spot also still burned from an earlier slam. Nothing in
   the game marked a dodge that works.
2. **At the golem's legs the camera loses the warrior, and the deflect looks like a core hit** (MAJOR).
3. **The colossus's impacts have no mass** (MINOR). The STRIKE tag appeared while the warrior was being thrown.
4. **The warrior's own attacks do not read** (MINOR).
5. **Lightning is a frozen squiggle** (MINOR). One channel hanging still through a strike; far over the mountains.

What feels good: "the storm sells the arena ... the core hit is the best beat in the set ... the heavy-charge glow and
the pale shockwave ring are readable at a glance."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 6 -> round 7)

- A dodge that works says so: a silver shimmer peels off the warrior and an EVADED word appears by the body (one per
  blow). DEFLECTED is larger and warm gold, so it never reads as the cyan core's flash.
- No STRIKE call while the warrior is down or reeling from a hit.
- Lightning: every return stroke of a strike takes a new channel on the same bearing (the bolt flickers), half the
  strikes land close, over the broken arches, and the flash lights the scene a little less (no overcast day look).
- The HUD stays laid out while hidden (opacity instead of display none), so showing it when the fight starts never
  stalls a frame.
- Capture: the shockwave beat polls every frame and rolls forward through the ring, on clean ground at full health;
  the shot log records the warrior's state.
