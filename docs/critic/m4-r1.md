# Critic report: milestone 4 (UI), round 1

Screenshots: `test-output/m4-critic-r1/` (`--scenario uicritic`). The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

What works (critic): one consistent type and button system (Cinzel headings and buttons, EB Garamond text, one
gold-lit primary button per screen); the death screen's cause and advice; a colour legend that matches the world.

1. **Stamina is never explained and the bars have no labels** (MAJOR). A thin unlabelled green line; a half-empty
   backing box.
2. **Low health looks like a mostly full bar** (MAJOR). A pale damage chip reads as health; no danger signal.
3. **STRIKE tag, damage numbers and victory framing overlap** (MINOR). The chevron sits 100 px above the core, numbers
   spawn on it or float over bare stone, the warrior stands behind the victory stats.
4. **Keys are tiny and named differently in the card** (MINOR). 8 px keycaps, "Q or Tab" in one cap, LMB versus
   Left click, no gamepad layout.
5. **Settings readouts contradict the controls** (MINOR). Sensitivity 100% with the handle a third along; a white
   browser checkbox.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 1 -> round 2)

- Health and Stamina labels on a tighter panel, a thicker stamina bar; How to fight has a Health and stamina section.
- The damage chip is dark blood and drains quickly; under 30% health the bar pulses, a blood-red edge closes in, and
  the R key and flask pips light up while a flask is left.
- The STRIKE chevron sits on the top of the core's glow at any distance; numbers rise from the hit point beside it and
  ride along with their core; the victory column moved to the left so the warrior stays in view.
- One keycap per key at 13 px, a Keyboard / Pad table, the same words in the card and in How to fight; the page fits
  a 720p screen without scrolling.
- Sensitivity reads as a multiplier (1.00x); the checkbox is drawn in the game's gold; values sit by their sliders.
