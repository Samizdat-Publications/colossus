# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 4

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r4/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Leap slam telegraph is hidden under the boss bar** (MAJOR). The camera tilts up to follow the leaping
   golem and pushes the player and the near half of the landing ring behind the boss panel.
2. **Up close, wind-ups are off-screen and the death is never explained** (MAJOR). At the golem's feet
   the camera shows only legs; sweeps have no ground marker, unlike slams and rocks.
3. **Phase 3 is visual noise** (MAJOR). Overlapping meteor rings, burning cracks everywhere, the meteor
   trail seems to come out of the golem, the player is a tiny dark figure, the burst chest is a pea-sized
   dot.
4. **Punish windows send mixed signals** (MINOR). Red crack veins under the stuck fist look like the
   burning hazard, so the right move looks like standing in fire.
5. **On-screen text covers the boss** (MINOR). Tip banner over the golem's head; controls card all phase 1.

Also: rock not visible in flight; victory collapse starts with arms up (reads as a wind-up); death-screen
camera inside the golem's forearm. What works: STRIKE markers, banners, damage numbers, BREAK meter,
clear end screens.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 4 -> round 5)

- Camera framing: the warrior and the near edge of every warning ring around them are now a hard
  constraint (the golem's top is soft); when both cannot fit the camera pulls back up to 10 m, FOV up to 80.
- Ground telegraphs for every melee attack: sweeps draw a red arc sector over the area the arm will
  cross, stomps draw a ring around the lifted foot, the stagger rise draws its push ring.
- Phase 3: 7 meteors instead of 11, rejection-sampled so rings never overlap, slanting in from the side;
  hazards last 8/10/12 s with at most 8 active; the molten chest core is three times larger and lights the
  golem; a soft light follows the warrior so they never vanish into the dark.
- Cold cracks under a stuck fist are dark fissures; they only glow orange in the last 0.8 s before burning.
- Tips moved to a side panel on the left; controls card is smaller and sits on the right edge.
- Victory collapse starts from a slump (no arms up); the death camera is pushed out of the golem.
