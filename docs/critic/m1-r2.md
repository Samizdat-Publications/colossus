# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 2

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r2/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The camera pushes into the golem or loses it completely** (MAJOR). Sweep shot sits on the warrior's
   back and cuts him off; double slam is pressed into a dithered arm with a clipped core glow; during the
   leap the golem is not in frame at all (only a red ring); floor rings run off the bottom of the screen.
2. **The golem is dark on dark, so its wind-ups can only be read from the floor** (MAJOR). Golem, walls,
   pillars and rubble are the same dark navy against a near-black sky with no edge light; rocks and meteors
   invisible in flight; phases barely change the look (no orange cracks, phase 3 chest is one small dot).
3. **Core hits show no progress, and the BREAK meter is invisible until it is full** (MAJOR). No empty
   track under the BREAK label; the punish-window core looks a body-length above the warrior's head.
4. **The HUD covers the action** (MINOR). Boss bar and tip banner sit where the golem's head and raised
   fists go; bottom panels cover floor hazards; controls line always on; "Lock on with Q" stays after
   lock-on.
5. **The kill and the death don't show what happened** (MINOR). Killing blow shows only legs; collapse
   off-screen; stray number next to the boss name; death with a beige 60% block on the health bar.

Cosmetic, not ranked: no rain, lightning or standing water; braziers are flat triangles; inactive cores
are unlit teal balls; HUD shows during the intro.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 2 -> round 3)

- Camera: pulled back and up (8 m, higher pivot and pitch); near the golem it rises instead of pushing
  in; the leap frames the airborne golem and its landing ring together; the victory cut pulls back to
  show the whole collapse.
- Readability: moonlight now comes from behind the golem with a cool rim light (Fresnel) on its stone,
  sky gradient dome, lighter golem stone; phase 2 and 3 use a stone shader with glowing orange crack veins
  and a lava light in phase 3; rocks and meteors are bigger and brighter with long trails.
- Progress: the BREAK track is always visible with a label; arm cores moved to the wrist so they sit at
  the warrior's head height in punish windows; critic run now includes a core-hit moment.
- HUD: boss bar at the bottom centre (Souls layout), player bars top left, tips in the upper middle,
  controls card only during the first minute of the first attempt (H toggles), lock-on tip hides once
  locked, HUD hidden until the intro roar.
- Death: the health trail drains at once on death; critic run uses a real death by golem.
