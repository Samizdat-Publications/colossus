# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 12

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r12/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Up close the colossus is an unreadable heap** (MINOR). Stomp and two-fisted slam read as a leaning tower of
   blocks; loose cubes hovering over the head (the back arch) read as stray geometry.
2. **Phase 3 does not look like the climax** (MINOR). The lava light is a tint on distant walls; no meteor in the
   sky in the meteor shot; the burst chest is one orange bulb.
3. **The warrior shrinks in the busiest moments, and the HUD covers a hazard** (MINOR). A landing ring under the
   boss name and bar.
4. **Storm effects and sword trails look flat** (MINOR). Lightning as a white scribble or paper streamer; the
   sword trail a grey slab.
5. **Rough edges around the cinematics and the death screen** (COSMETIC). The HUD over the intro cinematic;
   the fallen warrior hidden behind the stats box; a cyan floor glint under a core.

What works: "the fight explains itself well. Every attack shown has its own floor telegraph ... the rain-lashed
moonlit arena, the rubble rising, the low-angle roars and the clean title, victory and death screens give it a
confident Souls-like frame."

Worst remaining issue severity: MINOR

## Fixes (round 12 -> round 13)

- The back arch is gone from golem.py (it read as stray cubes); the column thighs stay.
- Phase 3: the molten pool is larger and brighter, and the heart has a big orange glow sprite spilling out of
  the burst chest. Meteors appear in the sky 1.8 s before they fall, lower (21 m), so the rain reads.
- The boss panel fades to 28% while a warning ring lies under it.
- Lightning is a glowing channel (a bright core with a soft halo across the ribbon), wider, with forks. The sword
  trail is faint at the hilt and brightest along the blade's edge, and follows the longer blade.
- The HUD appears only as the intro cinematic hands over to the fight. The cyan core light sits above its core
  and is softer (no second "core" on the wet floor). Vertical menus share one button width.
- Death camera: one continuous clock and viewpoint across dying and the FALLEN screen (the viewpoint used to be
  re-chosen when the screen appeared); it picks the clearest of 16 directions around the body, preferring the
  golem behind the body, re-picks (and glides) if a limb swings into view, frames the body in an outer third
  of the screen clear of the text, and fades golem limbs near the lens or across the view much harder.
