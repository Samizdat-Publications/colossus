# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 15

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r15/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The phase 3 camera and HUD lose the fight** (MINOR). A foreground column covered a side of the frame in the
   meteor rain; the boss bar faded to almost nothing under a ring; the leap cropped at the top.
2. **Phase 3 lighting turns the climax pink and sandy** (MINOR). A salmon golem, a sandstone floor the same amber
   as burning ground.
3. **Sweep, fissure and shockwave warnings are the weakest shapes** (MINOR).
4. **The DOWN state has no creature in it** (MINOR). A heap of masonry; the cyan shaft like a loot beacon; an
   unexplained cyan floor ring.
5. **Up close the golem is an unreadable dark heap** (MINOR).

What works: "the UI around the fight is clear and consistent ... in phases 1 and 2 the colour language holds ... the
rubble assembly, the backlit roar and the phase 2 lightning roar sell the storm and the size of the golem."

Worst remaining issue severity: MINOR

## Fixes (round 15 -> round 16)

- Arena columns between the lens and the fight, or within about 3.6 m of it, dither away (the golem keeps its
  whole-part fade; the pillars get their own fade uniforms).
- The boss panel dims to 55% over a ring instead of 28%.
- Phase 3: the heart's light is much weaker on the golem's own chest (110, 160 open) and the floor pool is a smaller,
  dimmer, deep red, so the stone stays dark with glowing veins and the floor never reads as burning ground.
- DOWN: the shaft of light over the back core is half as tall and narrower; the cyan floor ring is gone.
