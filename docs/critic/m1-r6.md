# Critic report: milestone 1 (greybox), round 6

Screenshots: `test-output/m1-critic-r6/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The camera loses the warrior during the busiest attacks** (MAJOR). Wide pull-backs make the warrior a
   speck (double slam, meteors, leap); in punish windows the planted forearm fills the frame; framing
   swings wildly.
2. **Phases 2 and 3 break the "only the glowing cores" rule** (MAJOR). The phase 3 heart glows cyan-white
   but is unmarked; phase 2 cracks cover the whole body.
3. **The thrown rock is invisible** (MINOR). Only the ring under the warrior; the golem's arm is empty.
4. **The stagger is hard to read** (MINOR). Heap of boxes; no sign of how long the opening lasts.
5. **The burning-floor warning is confusing** (MINOR). Cracks under the punish window read as fire; the burn
   tip appears mid-sweep.

What works: phase 1 banners, STRIKE tags, damage numbers, both end screens.

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 6 -> round 7)

- Steadier camera: pull-back capped at 3 m (wide requests 4 m leap, 2 m stagger, 3 m victory), slower
  damping of pitch, field of view and distance; rocks in flight are soft framing points.
- The phase 3 heart glows lava-orange while it cannot be struck and turns cyan (with the STRIKE tag) only
  when the golem is down; phase 2/3 veins dimmer.
- A rock is visible in the golem's fist before the throw; release is later and lower (at most 10.5 m) so
  the flight stays in view.
- While the golem is down the BREAK bar becomes a countdown labelled DOWN.
- Danger clears any tip on screen; cracks under a stuck fist stay dark until the last 0.5 s.
