# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 4

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r4/` (first build with the milestone 3 storm and effects). The critic
saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The golem looks like stacked boxes, not a living ruin** (MINOR). Roars tipped the head back so the
   face and eyes vanished; gaps at the hips and knees; the high stagger view still read as a pile.
2. **The orange glow outshines the real weak points** (MINOR). Eyes, phase 2 cracks and the phase 3 heart
   are brighter than the small cyan cores.
3. **Leftover greybox shapes and flat effects** (MINOR). Fissure spikes were see-through orange cones;
   burning ground a flat decal; dust glowing like lanterns.
4. **You lose track of your own warrior** (MINOR). A pale knight on pale wet stone; the cape once flipped
   up over the head like a signboard.
5. **The arena looks neither drowned nor like night** (COSMETIC). Overcast brightness; water holes full of
   glitter; terracotta mountains in phase 3.

What works: "the red floor warnings, the tip strip, the STRIKE markers, the BREAK/DOWN meter and both end
screens make the damage loop and the win/lose flow clear. That's why nothing here is worse than MINOR."

Worst remaining issue severity: MINOR

## Fixes (round 4 -> round 5)

- Roars keep the face toward the arena (head tilt 34 to 12 degrees, 40 to 16 in the meteor roar).
- Stagger camera: a lower, wider 3/4 view (rise 5 to 2.2 m, pull-back 3.5 to 4.5 m) so the kneel reads.
- Boulders fill the hips and the backs of the knees.
- Arm cores 35% larger; eyes dimmer (4.5 to 3.2).
- Fissure spikes are jagged rock shards (the debris chunk stretched), not cones.
- Burning ground has tongues of flame (rising glow sprites) over the embers and smoke; dust is darker
  and more transparent.
- The knight's steel is darker (a dark figure on pale stone, bone cape); the cape can never rise above
  its shoulder row and the wind is gentler.
- Night: exposure 0.9, dimmer sky light; the water is a readable grey-blue with less glitter; phase 3
  tints the fog less.
- Milestone 4 menus are in: a designed title (text on a left vignette, the arena visible on the right),
  How to fight (loop, floor colours, controls), Settings (sensitivity, invert Y, volumes, camera shake,
  controls card; remembered), pause, and end screens with stats, in Cinzel and EB Garamond.
