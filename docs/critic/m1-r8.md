1. **Up close, the boss stops communicating and the floor markings do all the work** (MINOR). The sweep fan
   does not say which arm swings or which way; the shockwave ring does not say "roll or jump through";
   the collapsed heap has no readable front or back (greybox shapes).

5. **The arena from the pitch is not there** (COSMETIC). No rain, lightning, water; checkerboard floor;

What works: title, tips, STRIKE tag, BREAK/DOWN meter, cause-of-death advice, end screens; "the damage

Worst remaining issue severity: MINOR

## Fixes (round 8 -> round 9)

- Sweep arcs carry pulsing chevrons pointing the way the arm travels; the first shockwaves show "roll
  (Space) or jump (F) through the ring" (this tip is allowed during danger).
- Framing includes the nearest ring just behind the warrior; the boss panel backdrop is lighter.
- Arm cores burn brighter only while low enough to hit, dim otherwise; sealed cores are plain stone knobs;
  the phase 3 heart is a dim lava ember until it can be struck.
- Burning ground has a hard orange edge ring; damage numbers float above the glow on a dark backing; red
  edge flash when the warrior is hit; brighter warrior materials.
- Rocks are dark stone with an ember glow and an amber halo; the golem's eyes go dark on death.
