# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 16

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r16/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Close range turns into glare and rubble** (MINOR). The core-hit flash bleaches the warrior; the torso and the
   other arm merge above a punishing warrior; the kneeling golem has no findable head or limbs.
2. **The Living Ruin looks like a blocky robot** (MINOR). A stack of dark boxes backlit by the moon; phase 2 cracks
   read as a texture swap.
3. **The phase 2 fissure and stacked hazards are hard to read** (MINOR). No tip for the fissure; the sweep's edge
   buried under the arm and fire.
4. **Phase 3 does not flood the arena with lava light** (MINOR).
5. **Weak victory payoff and a hero who looks like a placeholder** (MINOR). The HUD stayed on over the victory
   collapse; the heavy sword trail a solid beige crescent.

What works: "the night arena sells itself: rain, lightning over moonlit arches, and the rubble rising rock by rock
into the roaring giant. The core loop is taught cleanly ... the title screen, HUD, FALLEN and RUIN SILENCED share
one confident font and palette, and each phase looks different at a glance."

Worst remaining issue severity: MINOR

## Fixes after round 16

- The player HUD hides during the victory collapse (as in the phase-change cut).
- The fissure has its own tip the first times it races out.
- The heavy attack's trail is warmer and more transparent.

## Milestone 2 closed

Closed after 16 rounds. Rounds 12 to 16 all came back MINOR at worst; the MAJORs of rounds 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10 and 11
(warrior visibility, stomp occlusion, the stagger frame, the phase 3 climax) were fixed. What keeps coming back is
not an asset defect: the golem is 15 m of dressed stone and boulders by design (a ruin, not a creature with a skin),
so at melee range it fills the frame, and the floor telegraphs carry the wind-ups. Framing, effects and phase
lighting move to the milestone 3 (feel) loop, the HUD and screens to the milestone 4 (UI) loop.
