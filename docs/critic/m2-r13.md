# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 13

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r13/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Up close, the colossus turns into an unreadable rock heap** (MINOR). The punish window pressed against its
   chest; the stagger a diagonal wall of masonry; the death shot down among its legs.
2. **The stomp shockwave is the faintest warning in the fight** (MINOR). A thin, see-through pale band, washed
   out by rain, the one threat you must time a roll against.
3. **The phase changes look like texture swaps** (MINOR). One even net of cracks on every block; phase 3 a slim
   stack of terracotta bricks with a white bulb for a chest and no visible eyes.
4. **The warrior is a small, pale figure with a cardboard cape** (MINOR). A flat khaki panel.
5. **Cheap-looking effects and one HUD overlap** (COSMETIC). The long phase 3 tip runs into the health panel;
   flat grey dust smears; crisp white rain rings like bubbles; rows of small flame cones; not flooded enough.

What works: "the whole package hangs together ... the colour language holds in every attack shown ... the rubble
assembling in the rain and the lightning-lit phase 2 roar with the warrior tiny at its feet deliver the mood and
scale the pitch promises."

Worst remaining issue severity: MINOR

## Fixes (round 13 -> round 14)

- Shockwave: a brighter, more saturated pale red band, 20% taller, nearly opaque while young, with a bright rim
  ring on the floor.
- Phase 2: cracks split some stones wide open while others stay whole (patchier coverage, wider veins).
- Phase 3: the heart is molten orange instead of white-hot, with a smaller glow; the eyes burn brighter.
- Cape: a dark border down both sides, a dark hem, and a faded circular emblem between the shoulders; the cloth
  lost its horizontal bend links so it can fold across its width.
- Cosmetic: the tip strip wraps (balanced, at most 560 px) instead of running into the health panel, and the
  phase 3 tip is shorter; dust is fainter; rain rings are fainter and softer.
