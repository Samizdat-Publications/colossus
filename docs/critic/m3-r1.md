# Critic report: milestone 3 (feel), round 1

Screenshots: `test-output/m3-critic-r1/` (bursts of consecutive frames around hits, impacts and weather, captured
with the simulation frozen for each frame). The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The melee camera is buried inside the golem** (MAJOR). The deflect beat was captured unlocked, the free camera
   between the golem's legs; the deflect itself a small glint and a few sparks, the label far from the blade.
2. **Getting hit shows no cause and no body reaction** (MAJOR). No shatter at the warrior on a rock hit, an idle
   pose at the moment of damage, a T-like knockback pose, and burning ground that only drains the bar.
3. **Your own attacks have no weight and no contact** (MAJOR). Inconsistent trails, no visible charge build-up, the
   core burst away from the blade, no golem reaction.
4. **Big impacts have no mass** (MINOR). A slam lands as a fog bank and pebbles.
5. **Lightning and fire look like flat graphics** (MINOR).

What feels good: "the storm does a lot of work ... the stomp shockwave is a bright ring you can't miss ... the core
hit is the best feedback in the build."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 1 -> round 2)

- Deflect: a fan of hot and white sparks, stone chips, a grit puff, a longer hit stop, a camera kick; the label stays
  by the blade. The capture now deflects while locked on, as a player would.
- Getting hit: a burst at the warrior's chest (sparks, a flash, dust, shattered stone for rocks and fists), a camera
  shake and kick by weight, a short hit stop, a stronger recoil pose held for 0.1 s, and a curled knockdown instead of
  flung arms. Burning ground: the screen flashes on each tick (every 0.35 s at most), embers and flame lick the legs,
  and the warrior flinches.
- Attacks: steadier light trails; the heavy charge crouches, heats the blade to a bright glow and draws motes into it
  (gold at full charge); the core burst sits where the blade meets the core; the golem's arm and chest jolt on hits.
- Slam: a fast, low dust wave, a slower cloud, more and higher chunks, a bigger splash.
- Lightning: a harder white pop (moonlight +9, sky light +2.2 at the peak), bolts reaching down to the ridge line.
  Flames have softer edges.
