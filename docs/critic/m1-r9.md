## Fixes (round 9 -> round 10)

- Meteor rain uses a player-focus framing: while rubble is falling the camera drops the golem's top as
  a constraint, moves in to 7.2 m and rises to look down on the warrior, so every ring around them and
  its timer disc is on screen and the warrior stays large.
- While the golem is airborne (leap) only its torso counts for the top of the frame, so the camera stops
  chasing raised arms off the top edge.
- After a phase-change roar the golem waits 1.8 s before its next attack (was 0.8 s). The critic capture
  now waits for the transition to start before taking the roar shot (the round 9 shot was taken before
  the roar began).
- The rest (warrior contrast, phase 3 chest burst, storm) belongs to milestone 2 (real models and
  textures) and milestone 3 (rain, lightning, particles).
