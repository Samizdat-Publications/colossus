# Critic report: milestone 3 (feel), round 2

Screenshots: `test-output/m3-critic-r2/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **Burn damage makes no signal, and harmless fire looks like harmful fire** (MAJOR). Fire drained the bar with no
   cue, while some patches with flames did no damage.
2. **The BREAK collapse has no scale and is hard to read** (MAJOR). The camera rose high and far; a few grey puffs;
   the STRIKE chevron and the lock-on diamond on different cores.
3. **The warrior's attacks have no weight or contact** (MINOR). The heavy release weaker than its own charge.
4. **The golem's blows and projectiles have no mass** (MINOR). A white glowing ball at a hit looked like a pickup.
5. **Deflect on stone is a whisper** (MINOR).

What feels good: "the storm does most of the work ... a lightning flash that lights the whole arena ... the core
hit is a real payoff ... the golden blade glow of the heavy charge reads at once."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 2 -> round 3)

- Burning: while the warrior stands in fire, a hot glow rises from the bottom of the screen, throbbing, and the
  health bar glows orange (it shows in any frame, not only as a flash). Flames stand only on patches that burn:
  the arming glow has no flames, and the last second of a patch is safe, dying embers with no flames.
- BREAK: the stagger camera is closer and lower (rise 3.4 m, widen 1.8 m); the knees hitting the floor throw a big
  burst of dust and stone with a heavy shake, kick and hit stop; while the golem is down the lock-on diamond sits
  on the core the STRIKE tag marks.
- Heavy attacks: the blade keeps its heat through the blow and flares on release; the strike kicks up grit and spray
  (and now plays its whoosh, which never fired before).
- Hits: no glowing ball at the warrior; a thicker dust wave at slams. Deflect: bigger sparks and a brighter flash.
  Lightning channels are thicker. The Camera shake setting scales hit kicks too.
