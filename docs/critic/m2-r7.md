# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 7

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r7/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **You cannot see yourself during the stomp** (MAJOR). The golem's planted fist and its core filled the
   middle of the frame and hid the warrior and the near edge of the shockwave ring the tip asks you to time.
2. **The phase 2 transformation is shot from the wrong angle** (MINOR). Side-on, head and arms off the top,
   the warrior under the boss bars.
3. **Phase 3 turns the floor into red-on-red noise** (MINOR). Red seams along the ring-shaped joints look like
   the red danger rings; the warrior small; the golem cropped in the meteor rain.
4. **The stagger pose is an unreadable heap** (MINOR). A huge forearm fills a third of the frame.
5. **Burning ground looks like flat orange stickers** (MINOR). No flame, smoke or firelight on the stone.

Also noted: the cape as a stiff corrugated sheet sticking out sideways; a grey core showing through the death
screen stats; a lone word on the hint's second line; no lightning in any frame; no standing water; the
fissure reads as a laser.

What works: "the rainy, moonlit arena, the rock-by-rock assembly and the Cinzel title, victory and death
screens give the game real mood. Phase 1 teaches its rule cleanly through consistent colours and tips."

Worst remaining issue severity: MAJOR

## Fixes (round 7 -> round 8)

- Line of sight: every golem bone whose capsule cuts the camera's view of the warrior's chest or feet fades to
  22% (segment-to-segment distance against the bone capsules), on top of the old near-camera fade.
- Phase cut: the camera goes in front of the golem's face (its facing, not the warrior's side), as far out as
  the pillar ring allows, and the lens is computed to frame feet to raised fists; the bars hide during the cut.
- Phase 3: the molten seep only glows within about 7 to 19 m of the golem (the heat is its own); the heart's
  light is stronger; the meteor rain gets the big-move pull-back.
- Stagger: the camera rises higher (3.8 m) to look down on the kneeling giant.
- Burning ground casts firelight: two pooled point lights follow the burning patches nearest the warrior. To
  pay for them, the three cyan core lights became one pooled light on the core that matters most (60 fps kept).
  Flame licks are larger and brighter.
- Cape: calmer wind and softer pleats. Death screen stats sit on a dark backing; hints wrap balanced.
- More lightning (every 4.5 to 12.5 s); shallow puddles (mirror-smooth, darker patches) on flat flagstones.
