# Critic report: milestone 2 (assets), round 6

Screenshots: `test-output/m2-critic-r6/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

1. **The camera can't keep the colossus in frame** (MINOR). Big moves cropped at the top (slam fist against
   the top edge, the thrown rock half out of frame, arms and head off the top in the meteor call, the phase 3
   leap as a pair of legs hanging from the top edge); up close the golem is a wall of blocks with no visible
   head; landed debris hides the edge of warning rings.
2. **Phase 3 turns into a mess of warm colours** (MINOR). Orange seams and whole flagstones glowing orange,
   hot cracked rocks all over the floor and an orange golem blur the danger colours; nothing says whether the
   orange tiles burn. The sealed chest core has the weak-point crystal shape but in lava orange.
3. **The red warnings look like flat stickers, and the one you must time is the faintest** (MINOR). Unlit solid
   paint; the fissure is a flat strip like a road line; the stomp shockwave is a thin pale line.
4. **The models look like primitive shapes** (MINOR). A stack of dark cubes with a small box head; the phase 2
   cracks are one even cell pattern; the thrown rock is a smooth maroon blob, meteors a cube and a pyramid; the
   warrior a jointed mannequin in close-up; the cape hangs like a flat board.
5. **Rain and rendering noise** (COSMETIC). Even, bright rain streaks near and far, through the boss name with
   no backing panel; rows of flat white dashes on the floor; dark blobs off the top edge of the title and
   victory screens; a developer credit line on the title.

Also noted: the dying shot showed the warrior still upright with an empty health bar (no hit reaction).

What works: "the mood lands straight away ... three phases that look instantly different: grey stone, then
orange cracks, then molten with a lava-lit floor. The teaching layer is good for a first attempt."

Worst remaining issue severity: MINOR

## Fixes (round 6 -> round 7)

- Framing: extra pull-back while a slam, double slam, throw or volley is wound up or a rock is in the air
  (4.5 m instead of 3), and a wide leap (widen 5 m, pull-back up to 9 m): the whole airborne golem now
  hangs over its landing ring. The chest shards that burst off in phase 3 sink into the floor after 1.5 s.
- Phase 3 colour: the molten water is a dim deep-red seep (well below the amber of burning ground); the
  sealed heart is a small ember deep in the chest, and only the open heart is the big cyan crystal. The
  phase 3 stagger bows low enough that the heart is really within reach (tested: light and heavy hits land).
- Warnings: landing rings are one shader with a frayed band, bright rim, noisy filling disc and a glow that
  spills onto the stone; the fissure is a dim red band with a jagged glowing crack tearing along it; the
  shockwave band is brighter, has a crest line at the height that hits, and dust rolls along its front.
- Models: the thrown rock and the meteors are rebuilt in Blender as dented, displaced boulders (subdivided
  hull pushed by noise) and lose the maroon glow; the cape has shaded pleats.
- Cosmetic: flagstone bevels are matte (no rows of moonlit dashes); rain fades with distance; the boss name
  and bars sit on a soft dark backing; the title camera orbits inside the pillar ring (the dark blobs were
  pillar capitals) and the victory and death cameras stay inside it; no developer credit on the title.
- Death: a recoil pose first, less slow motion (0.55 instead of 0.35), so the fall reads at once.
