# Critic report: milestone 3 (feel), round 8

Screenshots: `test-output/m3-critic-r8/`. The critic saw only the screenshots and the pitch.

What works (critic): the weather sells the place; burning ground, the pale shockwave band with EVADED, the cyan core
burst with its number and the BREAK fill, and the victory crumble all land.

1. **Camera buried in the golem's shin, warrior invisible** (BLOCKER). The deflect frames show a cyan fist core and
   DEFLECTED, but hardly any warrior; the sparks seem to come from the core.
2. **The thrown rock hits with nothing visible** (MAJOR). A white flash and lost health, no rock, no burst, no fall.
3. **The dodge roll does not look like a roll** (MAJOR). An upright ghost as the ring passes.
4. **Light swings have no arc and no contact** (MAJOR). Thin grey trails, no step, the blade away from the core burst.
5. **The heavy release is a flash, not a blow** (MAJOR). Charge and release show the same pose; nothing hits the floor.

Also seen: the kneeling golem reads as a heap; the warrior stands in burning cracks through the stagger; lightning
stops in mid-air; black unlit slam debris.

Worst remaining issue severity: BLOCKER

## Fixes (round 8 -> round 9)

- Roll: a full somersault that stays on the floor (the handoff fix: `Step.blendIn`, a pivot at the tucked body's
  centre, and a support table measured from the meshes over tuck weight and angle), about an axis tilted 32 degrees
  toward forward, so it is a shoulder roll that topples visibly sideways from behind (a plain forward somersault is
  end-on to the usual camera). The dodge shimmer starts outside the body, so the tumble is not a white ghost.
- Deflect: a hanging fist is a heap of blocks well outside its bone capsule, so it never counted as blocking the view.
  Fists and forearms get a wider line-of-sight margin; the spark fan is mostly white steel on grey stone. The capture
  lets the lock-on camera settle after its teleport (the r8 frame caught the camera still swinging).
- Rock and fist hits: a flash, a wide ring of larger fragments and a dust burst around the body (the impact is on the
  warrior's front, which the camera behind cannot see); a knockdown snaps the body back and round before it falls, and
  it lies twisted onto one shoulder.
- Light swings: brighter, longer trails that carry a short follow-through; longer lunges (0.65 m, 0.95 m on hit 3).
- Heavy: the blade comes down at once on release (`outCubic`), and the chop ends in a `heavyLand` impact: a crack scar
  in the flagstones, chips, grit, steel sparks and a camera shake.
